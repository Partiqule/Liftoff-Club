Name
Liftoff Club

Subtitle
Launches and the night sky

Promotional text
A family companion for rocket launches and the night sky. Look up together from your town.

Description
Look up together. Liftoff Club shows the public launch board, a plain answer about whether you might see a flight from your town, and the night sky passes that are worth stepping outside for.

Kids mode is on until a grown up turns it off. Quiet hours keep alerts from arriving at bedtime. The home screen widget shows the next launch. A saved list stays on the phone when the connection drops.

Liftoff Club is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any launch provider or agency. Company and agency names only identify launches and belong to their owners.
